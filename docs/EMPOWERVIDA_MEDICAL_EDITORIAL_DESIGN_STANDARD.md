# EmpowerVida Medical Editorial Design Standard
Status: Approved creative direction, 4 November 2026
Applies to: all new articles, article revisions, scientific illustrations, landing pages, social graphics and future site redesign work.
Owner: EmpowerVida editorial team.

## North star
The scientific credibility of a medical publication, the visual sophistication of a premium magazine, and the readability of excellent health journalism. Premium biomedical editorial, never generic wellness-blog stock art.

## Visual language
- Palette: deep forest green, midnight blue, teal and restrained turquoise, balanced with warm whites and subtle neutral backgrounds. Maintain WCAG AA contrast, especially over imagery.
- Imagery: sophisticated anatomically credible 3D medical/scientific visuals; restrained cinematic lighting; realistic texture and depth; editorial rather than sci-fi, sensational or gimmicky.
- Typography: bold, elegant headline hierarchy; comfortable readable body size and line length; ample paragraph and section spacing; strong contrast.
- Composition: calm, uncluttered hero image with protected headline/eyebrow space; no competing labels or cropped diagrams behind text.
- Visual rhythm: one hero plus approximately 2–3 explanatory illustrations for a ~1,800–2,200-word feature when useful; no mandatory image quotas. Use original diagrams only when they improve comprehension.
- Medical graphics: scientifically reviewed, with accurate anatomy and mechanisms. Distinguish hypotheses, associations and proven causal relationships. Captions and alt text explain rather than overclaim.
- Mobile first: design at 320/375/390px and desktop; no horizontal overflow, tiny baked-in text or inaccessible contrast. Responsive sizing and cropping, fast-loading optimized assets.
- Article structure: short standfirst, optional 'In 30 seconds' summary and jump navigation for long reads, generous whitespace, clear H2/H3 hierarchy, pull-out evidence when valuable.
- Preserve accessibility and SEO: semantic headings, descriptive alt text, good performance, existing slugs and canonical URLs unless explicitly approved.
- Voice: human, accessible Australian English; evidence-led optimism; avoid hype, guarantees and medical misinformation; AHPRA/TGA-aware editorial review.

## Article visual workflow
1. Outline mechanisms that benefit from illustration; specify scientific accuracy and accessibility requirements.
2. Create hero and explanatory graphics using consistent art direction, but tailor subjects to each article.
3. Review scientific accuracy, label legibility, responsive crop, contrast, image size and alt text.
4. Preview desktop/mobile, verify links/build, and obtain editorial approval before merging to main.
5. Apply design progressively to existing articles, not through an unreviewed site-wide overwrite.

## Mandatory article opening summary — “In 30 Seconds”
- Every new EmpowerVida blog article must include an “In 30 Seconds” summary immediately after the standfirst/introduction and before the main body.
- Use three or four concise, plain-English takeaways: what readers need to know, why it matters, and practical implications where supported by evidence.
- Make each summary specific to its article, clinically accurate, non-sensational and accessible to readers without scientific training.
- Design as a restrained, high-contrast editorial element with generous spacing, not a loud coloured callout. Ensure mobile readability.
- Add to existing articles progressively during their scheduled editorial reviews; do not automatically rewrite or publish unreviewed posts.
- Do not mistake the summary for medical advice or overstate early-stage evidence.
