### 1. Fasting Insulin (Not just Glucose)
*   **The Code:** `INS` or `Insulin, Fasting`
*   **Why:** Glucose is a lagging indicator. Your pancreas can mask insulin resistance for *years* by pumping out massive amounts of insulin to keep glucose normal. By the time glucose rises, the damage is done. Insulin rises first.
*   **The Trap:** Most doctors only order `HbA1c` or `Fasting Glucose`.
*   **Normal Range:** < 25 mIU/L (This is shockingly high)
*   **Empowervida Optimal:** **< 5 mIU/L**

### 2. ApoB (Apolipoprotein B)
*   **The Code:** `ApoB`
*   **Why:** Far more accurate than LDL Cholesterol. ApoB counts the actual *number* of atherogenic particles in your blood. You can have "low" LDL but high particle count (discordance), leaving you at risk.
*   **The Trap:** Focusing solely on "Total Cholesterol."
*   **Normal Range:** < 100 mg/dL
*   **Empowervida Optimal:** **< 60 mg/dL** (for regression of plaque)

### 3. hs-CRP (High-Sensitivity C-Reactive Protein)
*   **The Code:** `hs-CRP` or `Cardio CRP`
*   **Why:** The measure of systemic inflammation. It tells us if your arterial walls—and your body—are "on fire." Inflammation is the driver of almost all chronic disease.
*   **The Trap:** Standard "CRP" is not sensitive enough for cardiovascular risk. Must specify "hs-CRP."
*   **Normal Range:** < 3.0 mg/L
*   **Empowervida Optimal:** **< 0.5 mg/L**

### 4. Homocysteine
*   **The Code:** `HCY`
*   **Why:** A marker of methylation status. High levels indicate poor B-vitamin processing (MTHFR issues) and are directly toxic to the endothelium (blood vessel lining) and the brain.
*   **The Trap:** Often ignored unless you have had a stroke.
*   **Normal Range:** < 15 µmol/L
*   **Empowervida Optimal:** **6 - 9 µmol/L**

### 5. GGT (Gamma-Glutamyl Transferase)
*   **The Code:** `GGT`
*   **Why:** The most sensitive liver enzyme for oxidative stress. It is an early warning system for fatty liver and glutathione depletion (your body's master antioxidant).
*   **The Trap:** Doctors look at ALT/AST, which only rise when liver cells are actively dying. GGT rises when the liver is *stressed*.
*   **Normal Range:** < 60 U/L
*   **Empowervida Optimal:** **< 20 U/L**
