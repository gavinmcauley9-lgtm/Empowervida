# Internal Linking Strategy - EMPOWERVIDA

## 🎯 **Why Internal Linking Matters for SEO**

Internal links are one of the most underutilized SEO tools. They:
- **Distribute page authority** (like passing SEO "juice")
- **Help Google understand** site structure and hierarchy
- **Improve crawl efficiency** (Google finds all pages faster)
- **Increase dwell time** (users click through more pages)
- **Boost conversion** (guide users to protocols/shop)

**Goal:** Create a web of relevant, strategic links that guide both Google and users to your most important pages.

### **4. Blog Posts → Must Link to Protocols + Guides**

**Every blog post should include:**

1. **1-2 Links to Pillar Content** (Guides)
   - Example: "Learn more in our [Insulin Story](/insulin-story)."

2. **1-2 Links to Protocols** (Conversion)
   - Example: "Get the complete [Metabolic Stack](/protocol#metabolic-stack)."

3. **1 Link to Diagnostics** (Service)
   - Example: "Test your levels with [these labs](/diagnostics)."

4. **1-2 Links to Related Blog Posts**
   - Example: "Also read: [5 Signs of Insulin Resistance](/blog/23)."

**Pattern:**
- Intro → Link to related guide
- Middle → Link to protocol or related blog
- End → CTA linking to protocol
- Sidebar → "Related Posts" with 3-5 links

## 📝 **Anchor Text Strategy**

### **✅ Good Anchor Text (Use These):**

**Exact Match (sparingly):**
- "insulin resistance"
- "mitochondrial health"
- "longevity protocol"

**Partial Match (preferred):**
- "learn about insulin resistance"
- "optimize mitochondrial function"
- "physician-designed longevity stack"

**Branded:**
- "Metabolic Health Stack"
- "Vitality Protocol"
- "EMPOWERVIDA Longevity Guide"

**Natural/Contextual:**
- "this comprehensive guide"
- "our physician-designed protocol"
- "the complete stack"

### **❌ Avoid:**
- "Click here" (wastes SEO value)
- "Read more" (too generic)
- Over-optimized keywords (Google penalty risk)

## 📊 **Internal Link Metrics to Track**

**In Google Analytics:**
1. **Navigation Summary** (Behavior → Site Content → All Pages → Navigation Summary)
   - See which pages users click FROM a given page
   - Optimize links on pages with high traffic but low exits to key pages

2. **User Flow** (Behavior → Behavior Flow)
   - Visualize how users navigate your site
   - Find broken paths to protocols/shop

**In Google Search Console:**
3. **Internal Links Report** (Links → Internal Links)
   - See which pages have most internal links pointing to them
   - Goal: Protocol page should be in top 3
