The Metabolic 5

Labs Your Doctor Isn't Testing (But Should Be)
Dr. Gavin's Note: Standard reference ranges are designed to identify disease, not health. They are statistical averages of a sick population. If you want average health, use their ranges. If you want optimal longevity, use ours.
1

Fasting Insulin

The Early Warning System. Glucose is the crime scene; Insulin is the detective. Insulin rises years before blood sugar does. High insulin drives aging, vascular damage, and fat storage.
Standard Range (The "Sick" Zone) < 25 µIU/mL
Optimal Range (The Goal) 2.0 - 5.0 µIU/mL
2

ApoB (Apolipoprotein B)

The True Particle Count. Standard LDL-C ("Bad Cholesterol") estimates volume, like measuring traffic by weighing the cars. ApoB counts the actual number of crash-prone vehicles. It is the single best predictor of cardiac risk.
Standard Range < 100 mg/dL
Optimal Range < 60 mg/dL
3

High-Sensitivity CRP (hs-CRP)

The Fire Alarm. Chronic inflammation is the root of almost all modern disease. hs-CRP measures systemic inflammation in your arteries. If this is high, your cholesterol is far more dangerous.
Standard Range < 3.0 mg/L
Optimal Range < 0.5 mg/L
4

Homocysteine

The Methylation Marker. Elevates when your B-vitamin methylation pathways are jammed. High levels damage arterial linings and are strongly linked to dementia and stroke risk.
Standard Range < 15 µmol/L
Optimal Range 5.0 - 7.0 µmol/L
5

GGT (Gamma-Glutamyl Transferase)

The Oxidative Stress Gauge. Often ignored as just a "liver enzyme," GGT is actually a sensitive marker for total body oxidative stress and glutathione depletion. It predicts all-cause mortality better than almost any other enzyme.
Standard Range < 60 U/L
Optimal Range < 20 U/L
