### **3. Optimize Image Alt Text (SEO + Accessibility)**

**Current:** Some images have alt text
**Goal:** Every image has descriptive, keyword-rich alt text

**Examples:**

❌ Bad: `alt="image1"`
✅ Good: `alt="Insulin resistance progression showing three stages from normal to decompensated failure with mitochondrial dysfunction"`

❌ Bad: `alt="supplement"`
✅ Good: `alt="CoQ10 ubiquinol supplement for mitochondrial energy production and cellular health"`

**Priority images:**
- Hero images
- Blog thumbnails
- Infographics (like your new insulin resistance diagram)
- Protocol supplement images
